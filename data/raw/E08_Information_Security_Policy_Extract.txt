Section 4.1: Cryptographic Secrets & Credential Management
- All production API keys, tokens, and database passwords must be stored in an authorized Secrets Vault.
- Under NO circumstances may credentials or secrets be transmitted via email, chat, support tickets, or screenshots.
- Production service credentials must enforce an automatic rotation lifecycle (maximum TTL: 90 days) or possess a documented rotation schedule.

Section 4.2: Third-Party & Vendor Integrations
- All integrations must adhere strictly to the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP). Granted API permissions must match the explicit documented business purpose.
- Where supported by the protocol, vendor integrations must enforce network source restrictions (IP whitelisting or CIDR pinning).

Section 5.3: Bulk Learner Data Exports
- Bulk exports of learner Personally Identifiable Information (PII) require prior documented business justification and approval from Department Head or Data Protection Officer.
- Automated bulk export jobs must be logged and monitored for unauthorized distribution.

Section 8.2: Account Deprovisioning & Offboarding
- Access privileges of terminated or resigned employees must be revoked and their accounts disabled in the Identity Provider on or before their final working day.
