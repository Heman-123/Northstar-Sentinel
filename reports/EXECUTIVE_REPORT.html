1. Executive Summary

On 12 October 2026, Northstar Skills Academy observed degraded attendance synchronization, severe portal response latency surging from 45 ms to 8,420 ms (~187× spike), and an unexpected 486,200-byte learner export dataset staged within CampusVault S3 storage.

The Northstar Sentinel defensive platform ingested 12 evidentiary artifacts (E01–E12), verified bit-level SHA-256 integrity, normalized 40 events into OCSF schema, and established that vendor service credential rollcall-prod (svc_rollcall) was utilized from an unauthorized external public IP (203.0.113.77) to search, export, and exfiltrate 2,300 learner PII records.

ROOT CAUSES:
1. Scope Creep: Integration granted bulk export rights outside documented attendance scope (E05:INT-01).
2. Credential Hygiene: Token rc-prod-01 had Expiry: Never with no scheduled rotation (E08:Sec 4.1).
3. Ingress Absence: Gateway lacked CIDR subnet pinning (IP Restriction: None).
