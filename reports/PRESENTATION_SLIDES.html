[02] Problem Identification & Attack Landscape

INCIDENT DISCOVERY

  • Attendance Delay: Third-party connector experienced unexpected synchronization delays.
  • Portal Latency Surge: Student query latencies jumped from normal 45ms to 8,420 ms (~187× spike).
  • Data Staging in Cloud: Discovery of unscheduled 486.2 KB export file (learners-20261012.csv) in CampusVault S3 storage.

  • Rate Limiting Absence (OWASP API4 / CWE-770): No rate limiting, enabling unthrottled table scans of 2,300 learner records.
